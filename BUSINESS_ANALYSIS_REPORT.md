## Business Questions
1. Which Pokemon have the strongest total base-stat profiles?
2. Which Pokemon types have the largest representation?
3. How does Pokemon composition change across generations?
4. How do legendary and non-legendary groups compare?
5. Which Pokemon have the highest Attack, Defense and Speed?
6. Which types have stronger average total stats?

## Limitation
All findings are derived from the supplied workbook. No external battle results, revenue, engagement or commercial data has been added.
